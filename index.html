09 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

The architecture is there
for a reason.

Why Kafka?

Decouples producers from downstream consumers and lets multiple services react to the same business event.

Why CQRS?

Separates command-side business operations from read-side application views so each can evolve independently.

Why Event Sourcing?

Preserves a durable sequence of state-changing events and enables state reconstruction and replay.

Why RLS?

Moves tenant isolation into the database boundary instead of relying only on application-level filtering.

Why Outbox?

Coordinates database state changes with reliable event publication and reduces dual-write failure modes.

Why HITL?

High-risk autonomous actions stop for explicit authorization before a side effect is committed.
